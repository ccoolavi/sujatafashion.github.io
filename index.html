Frequently Asked Questions

What are the prerequisites for joining?
No prior experience required. Basic education (10th pass) is preferred. Passion for fashion and creativity is most important.
Do you provide certificates?
Yes, we provide completion certificates for all our courses. Our diploma course certificate is recognized in the fashion industry.
What is the batch size?
We maintain small batch sizes (8-12 students) to ensure personalized attention and hands-on learning experience.
Do you help with business setup?
We provide guidance for setting up your own business and connect you with our network of alumni and industry contacts.
Can I take multiple courses?
Yes, you can combine courses. We offer flexible scheduling and discounts for multiple course enrollments.

Online Course FAQ

How do live online sessions work?
Live sessions are conducted via video call. You can see the instructor's demonstrations in real-time, ask questions, and get instant feedback. Sessions are also recorded so you can review them later.
What equipment do I need for online courses?
A smartphone or laptop with internet connectivity is sufficient. For embroidery courses, you'll need basic Aari tools which we can help you source locally.
Can I switch from self-paced to instructor-led?
Yes, you can upgrade at any time. The fee difference is adjusted pro-rata based on your progress.
Do online students get the same certificate as offline?
Yes. The Sujata Fashion Academy certificate is awarded to all students who complete the course requirements, regardless of mode of study.
Is there a trial class available?
Yes, we offer a free trial session for all online courses. Contact us via WhatsApp to schedule yours.
