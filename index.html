Frequently Asked Questions

What are the prerequisites for joining?
No prior experience required. Basic education (10th pass) is preferred. Passion for fashion and creativity is most important.
Do you provide certificates?
Yes, we provide completion certificates for all our courses. Our diploma course certificate is recognized in the fashion industry.
What is the batch size?
We maintain small batch sizes (8-12 students) to ensure personalized attention and hands-on learning experience.
Do you help with business setup?
We provide guidance for setting up your own business and connect you with our network of alumni and industry contacts.
Can I take multiple courses?
Yes, you can combine courses. We offer flexible scheduling and discounts for multiple course enrollments.

For Partner Delivery:
The item will be delivered to your location by 4:00 PM on the rental start date.

For Own Pickup/Drop:
You may pick up your item any time after 12:00 PM (noon).
You must drop off the item before 12:00 PM (noon) on the following day.

A fixed deposit of ₹1500 and one valid ID proof are required—this deposit is non-negotiable and will be fully refunded, subject to return conditions.

The deposit refund will be processed to your requested payment source (same or other) only after these checks:

  • Timeliness: If the item is not returned within 24 hours, an extra one day’s rent will be deducted.
  • Integrity: The item must be returned in full; missing items will incur appropriate charges.
  • Condition: Any damage, visible stains (especially dark colors), or if the item is rendered unusable will result in forfeiture of the full deposit amount.
