Data Privacy & Recruitment Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Data Collection & Purpose

Sujata Fashion collects personal information (resumes, contact details) exclusively for the purpose of recruitment and course enrollment. We operate on a Data Sovereignty model:

  • No Third-Party Storage: We do not store, process, or retain any applicant data within third-party platforms.
  • Direct Application: All recruitment data is transmitted directly to our secure systems.

2. LinkedIn Integration & Compliance

  • Non-Automated Recruitment: We do not use automated scripts or AI-driven decision-making to screen applicants.
  • Manual Oversight: All hiring and enrollment decisions are made by human personnel.

3. Candidate Rights

  • 10-Day Deletion Rule: We commit to permanently deleting applicant data within 10 business days upon request or when no longer considered.
  • Opt-Out: Applicants can opt out of communications at any time by emailing privacy@sujatafashion.in.
